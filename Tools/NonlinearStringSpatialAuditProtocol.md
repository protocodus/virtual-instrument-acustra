# Fixed initial-state spatial audit, 2026-09-08

Frozen before calculation. This is independent of the fixed 13-coordinate
moving-port protocol and cannot rescue its gates. No trajectory, recording,
engine, calibration or sound-quality claim is part of this audit.

Use the original nonlinear prototype's plain nylon G3 physical constants,
Gaussian-smoothed triangular displacement at p=.173 and sigma/L=.012, and
amplitudes 1/2/4 mm. Study N=12/24/48/96/192 sine modes. Integrate the same
geometrically exact local nonlinear potential with 8N trapezoidal intervals;
repeat with 16N only as an independent quadrature check. The 192-mode static
state is a spatial reference, not a legal audio-rate dynamical model.

Report physical linear plus nonlinear initial energy, fixed-boundary point
force and Galerkin weak reaction, retained physical mode frequencies, and the
fraction of the 192-mode linear modal energy excluded at each truncation. Also
report the endpoint force and slope of the analytic unsmoothed triangular
string as a labelled comparison, not as the target for the smoothed stiff
string. Never change amplitudes, width, position or coefficients to obtain a
desired convergence result.

The energy/weak-reaction quadrature comparisons must agree between 8N/16N to
relative 1e-8 (absolute floor 1e-14 J / 1e-10 N). No acoustic acceptance
threshold is assigned. Report weak versus point-force difference separately
from their convergence toward the 192-mode state. Report exact counts of
physical modes below 3/5/10/15/20 kHz and Nyquist at 48/96/192 kHz, including
minimum uniform quadrature size 2N+1 under the prototype's finite-mode check.

This audit can identify bandwidth and spatial-closure limitations before a
full-band implementation. It cannot prove realistic excitation, sufficient
nonlinear bandwidth during a trajectory, runtime cost, or guitar superiority.
