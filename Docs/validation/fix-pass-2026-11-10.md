# Fix-only pass after 1.9.40 — 2026-11-10

A second read of the shipping model for defects only: no new control,
mechanism or preset, and no re-voicing. Sources from 1.9.40 (`ed26dd8`);
48 kHz, Release, gcc 13.3 unless a line says otherwise, on one shared
development machine. It starts from the
[consolidation audit](consolidation-audit-2026-11-09.md).

## The parameter matrix's silence after release

**Found.** Since 1.9.36 the Linux matrix failed six cases, all preset 3
(Jumbo Steel Pick) with Picking = 0 (fingers): −59.89 to −59.99 dBFS left
2.06 s after release, against the matrix's shared −60 dBFS.

**Diagnosis.** The limit was at fault, not the release. The matrix phrase (a C
major triad held 0.8 s) played through the Performer as the plug-in plays it,
48 kHz, block 64, peak of the 250 ms after the bound:

| Jumbo Steel Pick, fingers | Tail at the bound |
| --- | ---: |
| 1.9.35 (`f39bdc6`), Output −0.9 dB | −60.76 dBFS |
| 1.9.36 (`3452312`) and 1.9.40, Output −0.1 dB | −59.96 dBFS |
| 1.9.40, sympathetic strings off | −240 dBFS |
| 1.9.40, bridge coupling off | −110 dBFS |
| 1.9.40, note-offs lost | −42.3 dBFS |

1.9.36 re-trimmed that preset's Output by +0.8 dB to keep its picked level;
the engine under fingers is byte-identical, so the same tail came out 0.8 dB
louder. What sounds at the bound is not the released chord. The played
strings are at −88 dBFS 0.4 s after release and below −143 dBFS at the bound
on every preset and technique. It is the strings the chord left open, ringing
in sympathy through the bridge, which no hand damps
([README](../../README.md#bridge-sympathetic-strings-and-body)): −49.7 dBFS
1.2 s after release, a beat plateau near −51 dBFS to 1.5 s, −58.8 at 2.1 s and
−60.8 at 2.3 s on the Jumbo. Before Output that ring was −59.86 dBFS in
1.9.35 too; only the preset's −0.9 dB trim kept it under the floor.

Across the eight presets and three techniques the ring at the bound, before
Output, runs from −89.4 dBFS (Open G Slide-ready, pick) to −58.5 dBFS (Jumbo,
fingers); a lost note-off leaves −42.9 dBFS or more.

**Fix.** `Tests/MatrixRelease.h` sets Acustra's floor for that bound to
−54 dBFS (4.5 dB above the loudest ring before Output, 11 dB under the
quietest stuck chord) with the measurements beside it, and
`Tests/ParameterMatrixTests.cpp` uses it. The two-second bound and the
lost-note-off rejection are unchanged. No engine change, no version change.

**Test.** `Acustra.MatrixRelease` (unit, JUCE-free) plays the matrix's chord
on every preset with every technique and requires three things: the played
strings below −120 dBFS at the bound with the sympathetic strings off, the
open strings' ring under the floor before Output, and a lost note-off at
least 6 dB above it. At the old floor it fails on the Jumbo with fingers
(−58.47 dBFS) and thumb (−59.84 dBFS).
